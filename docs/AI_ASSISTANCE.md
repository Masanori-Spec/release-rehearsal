# AI assistance and provenance

AI assistance was used for problem research, scope selection, implementation, interface copy, test generation, independent review, and documentation. The product has no runtime AI dependency. The source, tests, fixture inputs, version lock and verification status are included to make the work inspectable.

Automated checks and AI source review can miss errors. A passing fixture demonstrates only its stated scenario. A portfolio presentation should distinguish what its presenter has personally understood or reproduced from AI-assisted construction. There are no claimed customers, interviews, time savings, incident reductions, patents or originality guarantees.

The UI's three quick demos use synthetic file names, sizes and SHA-256-shaped values. They are explicitly labeled; they are not measured Vite outputs. `fixtures/generated` contains separate manifests and inventories measured from the pinned official Vite fixture builds. Reports exported from quick demos include synthetic input context.

No private user data, university systems, customer correspondence, account credentials or external uploaded application source were used. The repository does not introduce a license decision on the owner's behalf. Dependency licenses remain those supplied by their respective packages.
